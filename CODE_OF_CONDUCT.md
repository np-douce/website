# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our community is dedicated to providing a welcoming environment for everyone. All participants are expected to be respectful, kind, and considerate.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hateful comments, persistent or targeted abuse, threats, or other actions that make contributors feel unsafe. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue (private if necessary) addressed to the maintainers or contact the repository owner.

By participating you agree to follow this code of conduct. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, block users, and take other measures to keep the community safe.
